Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Who do you work with?

We focus on small and growing businesses, typically those with 5–50 employees, that need practical HR or recruitment support.

Do you replace an internal HR department?

Our services are designed for businesses that don't have dedicated HR capacity, or need additional support when their existing team is stretched.

Can I hire you just for recruitment?

Yes. Recruitment can be engaged as a standalone service or as part of ongoing HR support.

Do you offer one-time HR projects?

Yes. HR setup, documentation, onboarding systems and other defined projects can be scoped separately.
